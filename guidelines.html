01 — Overview

Welcome to EduCoffee

EduCoffee is a digital platform designed to connect private teachers and students across Bangladesh. We provide tools for batch management, result publishing, notice broadcasting, and performance tracking — all in one place.

These guidelines exist to ensure the platform remains safe, productive, and fair for everyone. By creating an account, you agree to follow these guidelines in addition to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Important
Violation of these guidelines may result in account suspension, plan downgrade, or permanent removal from the platform without a refund.
02 — Eligibility

Who Can Use EduCoffee?

EduCoffee is open to:

  • Private tutors, coaching center teachers, and educators based in Bangladesh.
  • Students of any age who are enrolled in a teacher's batch via a valid batch code.
  • Parents or guardians who access the platform on behalf of their children.

You must provide accurate personal information during registration. Using false names, fake phone numbers, or invalid email addresses is a violation of these guidelines.

Age Requirement
Students under 13 years of age must have explicit parental consent. Teachers must be at least 18 years of age.
03 — User Roles

Understanding User Roles

EduCoffee has two primary roles. Each role has different permissions and responsibilities.

Teacher
  • Create and manage batches
  • Publish exam results
  • Broadcast notices to students
  • View performance analytics
  • Communicate with parents
Student
  • Join batches using a batch code
  • View exam results and remarks
  • Read teacher notices
  • Track personal performance
  • Enroll in multiple teachers' batches
04 — Teacher Guidelines

Guidelines for Teachers

As a teacher, you are responsible for the accuracy and appropriateness of all content you create on EduCoffee, including batches, results, and notices.

Batch Creation Rules

  • Batch codes must be 6 characters and should be shared only with your enrolled students.
  • Do not create batches for classes you do not actually teach.
  • Batch names should clearly identify the class level and session (e.g., "Class 9 — Morning Batch").
  • You may create multiple batches for the same class with different schedules.
  • Do not use batch codes to mislead or impersonate another teacher.

Publishing Results

  • Only publish results for exams you have genuinely conducted.
  • Marks must be accurate and reflect actual student performance.
  • Remarks should be constructive, professional, and appropriate for students and parents to read.
  • Do not mark a student as absent without valid reason.
  • Once published, results should not be deleted without informing students.

Posting Notices

  • Notices must be relevant to your students and their studies.
  • Do not use the notice system for personal promotions unrelated to education.
  • Urgent notices should only be marked as urgent when genuinely time-sensitive.
  • All notices must comply with our Code of Conduct.
Best Practice
Keep your center name, schedule, and contact information up to date so students and parents can easily reach you.
05 — Student Guidelines

Guidelines for Students

  • Only use batch codes provided by your actual teacher. Do not share batch codes with people not enrolled in that class.
  • Treat your teacher's notices and results with respect. Do not screenshot and publicly share private academic information without consent.
  • You may enroll in multiple teachers' batches using a single EduCoffee account.
  • Your account is personal. Do not share your login credentials with others.
  • If you see incorrect marks in your result, contact your teacher directly — do not attempt to alter platform data.
  • Report any inappropriate content from a teacher immediately using our contact channels.
06 — Code of Conduct

Code of Conduct

All EduCoffee users — teachers, students, and administrators — must adhere to the following standards:

  • Respect: Treat all users with dignity and respect, regardless of age, gender, religion, or background.
  • Honesty: Provide truthful information. Do not publish false results, fake notices, or misleading content.
  • Privacy: Do not share other users' personal information, including student scores and contact details, without consent.
  • Professionalism: All written content on the platform (remarks, notices, descriptions) must maintain an educational, professional tone.
  • Safety: Do not use EduCoffee to harass, threaten, or intimidate any user.
07 — Prohibited Actions

Prohibited Actions

The following are strictly prohibited on EduCoffee:

Strictly Prohibited
Violation of any of the following may result in immediate account termination.
  • Creating fake accounts or impersonating another teacher or student.
  • Using invalid or fraudulent Transaction IDs (TrxID) to obtain a paid subscription.
  • Attempting to access the accounts of other users without authorization.
  • Posting offensive, sexually explicit, or politically inflammatory content.
  • Using the platform to conduct any form of financial fraud or scam.
  • Reverse-engineering, scraping, or attempting to extract data from the platform's API without authorization.
  • Using automated bots or scripts to interact with the platform.
  • Sharing copyrighted educational content without proper rights or permissions.
  • Selling, transferring, or renting your account to another person.
08 — Subscription Rules

Subscription & Payment Guidelines

  • Payments must be made through the methods listed on our platform (bKash, Nagad, or in-person at our office).
  • The Transaction ID (TrxID) submitted during registration must be genuine and match the exact payment made.
  • Submitting a false TrxID to gain paid-plan access is fraudulent and will result in immediate downgrade and possible legal action.
  • If you pay at our office, you will receive a payment code that activates your account instantly.
  • Subscription plans are non-transferable between accounts.
  • EduCoffee reserves the right to downgrade or suspend accounts where payment cannot be verified.
  • Refund requests are handled on a case-by-case basis. Contact us within 3 days of payment for disputes.

Reward Program

  • Monthly book and gift rewards are given to the top-scoring student in each teacher's batch.
  • Rewards are at EduCoffee's sole discretion and are not guaranteed.
  • Rewards cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to another student.
  • The teacher must sign the acknowledgement receipt before the reward is shipped.
09 — Content Standards

Content Standards

All content published on EduCoffee — including notices, exam titles, descriptions, and remarks — must meet these standards:

  • Language: Content should be written in English or Bangla. Mixing languages is acceptable but must remain readable.
  • Tone: Educational and professional. Avoid slang, offensive language, or personal insults.
  • Accuracy: Do not publish information you know to be false or misleading.
  • Length: Notices are limited to 1,000 characters. Keep content concise and meaningful.
  • Relevance: All content must be relevant to the educational context of the batch it is sent to.
10 — Enforcement

Enforcement & Consequences

EduCoffee reserves the right to take action against any account that violates these guidelines. Actions may include:

  • Warning: A written warning sent to the account's registered email.
  • Temporary Suspension: Restricted access for a defined period (1–30 days).
  • Plan Downgrade: A paid plan may be reverted to Starter without refund.
  • Permanent Termination: Complete removal of the account and all associated data.

Users may appeal a suspension by contacting us at support@educoffee.app within 7 days. Appeals are reviewed within 5 business days.
