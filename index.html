✦ Features

Everything a teacher
& student needs

Built for the realities of private coaching in Bangladesh — no bloat, no complexity.

📚
Batch Management
Create unlimited batches, generate unique batch codes, set schedules, and manage enrollment effortlessly.
📊
Result Publishing
Upload exam scores for every student, add remarks, and publish with one click. Students see results instantly.
🔔
Notice Broadcasting
Send urgent or general notices to specific batches or all students. Mark as urgent with email notifications.
🧑‍🎓
Multi-Teacher Dashboard
Students connect with multiple teachers in one dashboard — no juggling between accounts or logging in/out.
📈
Performance Analytics
Visual trend charts for every student. Teachers spot who's improving and who needs attention at a glance.
🔐
OTP Verification
Secure email OTP during signup. Optional 2FA for teachers. Your data is protected at every step.
👨‍👩‍👧
Parent-Friendly Design
Guardians and students share the same dashboard — designed for both. Check notices & results on mobile.
📧
Parent Communication
Message individual parents or broadcast to all. Keep guardians informed about exams, results, and events.
✦ How It Works

Up and running
in 3 steps

EduCoffee is designed to get teachers and students connected without any technical hurdles.

01
🖊️
Teacher Signs Up
Register with email, choose a subscription plan, and set up your coaching center profile.
02
🔗
Students Join
Students enter the batch code from their teacher. They can join multiple teachers — all in one account.
03
🚀
Start Managing
Publish results, send notices, track performance, and communicate with parents — everything from one hub.
